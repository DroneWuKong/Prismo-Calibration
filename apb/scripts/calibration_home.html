1 · Tell us your setup

What will this change?

Calibration saves measurements, correction values and a report. Lens corrections are for camera software; camera/IMU alignment is for the estimator; optical-flow corrections are for flow processing; camera alignment is for vision software or mount adjustment.

Review the export before using it in another system. This app does not apply FC parameters or move a mount. Prismo live motion recording temporarily requests IMU telemetry and restores its rate on exit.

2 · Choose what to calibrate

All four workflows accept saved recordings. Prismo camera and camera/IMU guided recording requires the APB services. Sensors and cameras must be present in your actual setup; choosing a board does not add them.
